# The Conjuring (2013)

> "The dark presence is real." — Ed and Lorraine Warren

## Overview
**The Conjuring** is a chilling supernatural horror film directed by James Wan[cite: 1]. It follows paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren as they try to help the Perron family, who are experiencing increasingly disturbing paranormal events in their Rhode Island farmhouse.

### Key Details
* **Director:** James Wan
* **Release Year:** 2013
* **Main Characters:** 
  * Ed Warren (Patrick Wilson)
  * Lorraine Warren (Vera Farmiga)
  * Carolyn Perron (Lili Taylor)

### Famous Elements
1. **The Annabelle Doll** – Introduced in the terrifying opening sequence.
2. **The Clapping Game** – "Hide and Clap."
3. **The Haunting** – Based on the "true story" case files of the Warrens.
